# SpendSense
![SpendSense banner](images/banner.webp)

> *Make sense of your spending.*

Ever looked at a bank statement and wondered where the month went? SpendSense helps turn that question into something easier to answer.

SpendSense is a Databricks-based financial analytics project that reads account statements and budgets from Google Drive, cleans and categorises the data through Bronze, Silver, and Gold ETL pipelines, and brings the results to life in dashboards. It helps compare planned budgets with actual spending, while tracking income, outgoings, balances, and month-to-month changes.

For now, SpendSense is used to track and understand spending across a joint account.
